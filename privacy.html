Privacy Policy

Plot Twist Toons Uploader · Last updated: November 18, 2026

What this app is

Plot Twist Toons Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the Plot Twist Toons YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own content. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Information we access

  • The OAuth access granted by the channel owner, used to upload videos, edit video metadata, manage playlists, read and reply to comments on our own videos, and read our own channel analytics.
  • Public comments left on our own videos, read only to reply to and moderate them.

How we use and store it

Authorization tokens are stored privately by the channel owner and used only to perform the actions above on our own channel. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. Analytics are used only in aggregate to improve our own videos.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted within 30 days after access is revoked or the app is retired.
